# How to Use Notifications - User Guide

## Setting Up Notifications (When Scheduling)

When you schedule a supplement, you'll be asked about notifications:

1. **Go to Schedule tab**
2. **Select a date** on the calendar
3. **Tap "+ Add"** button
4. **Select a supplement** from your stack
5. **Choose a time** (or enter custom time like "14:30")
6. **Enter dosage** (optional, e.g., "2 capsules")
7. **Choose schedule type:**
   - Once (one-time)
   - Every Day
   - Specific Days (weekly)

8. **💬 Notification prompt:** "Do you want to be reminded to take this supplement?"
   - **Tap "No Reminder"** to skip notifications
   - **Tap "Remind Me"** to enable notifications ✅

9. **⏰ Choose reminder time:**
   - "At scheduled time" - Get notified exactly when scheduled
   - "30 minutes before" - Get notified 30 min early
   - "1 hour before" - Get notified 1 hour early

10. **Grant permission** when prompted (first time only)

## Managing Notifications (After Scheduling)

### Viewing Notification Status

On the Schedule tab, each scheduled supplement now shows:
- 🔔 **Bell icon with timing** if notifications are enabled
  - "🔔 At time" = Notification at scheduled time
  - "🔔 30 min before" = Notification 30 minutes early
  - "🔔 1 hour before" = Notification 1 hour early
- No bell icon = No notification set

### Editing Notifications

To add or change notifications for an existing schedule:

1. **Tap on any scheduled supplement**
2. **Select "Edit Notification"** or **"Add Notification"**
3. Choose from the options:

   **If notification is already enabled:**
   - "Change Timing" - Pick a different reminder time
   - "Turn Off" - Disable the notification
   - "Cancel" - Keep current settings

   **If notification is not enabled:**
   - "At scheduled time"
   - "30 minutes before"
   - "1 hour before"
   - "Cancel"

## Example Flow

### Example 1: Adding Vitamin D with 30-min reminder

1. Go to Schedule → Select today's date
2. Tap "+ Add"
3. Select "Vitamin D" from list
4. Enter time: "08:00"
5. Enter dosage: "2 capsules"
6. Choose: "Every Day"
7. Prompt: "Do you want to be reminded?" → **Tap "Remind Me"**
8. Choose: **"30 minutes before"**
9. ✅ Done! You'll see: "🔔 30 min before" on the card

### Example 2: Turning off notifications

1. Go to Schedule → Tap on "Vitamin D"
2. Select **"Edit Notification"**
3. Tap **"Turn Off"**
4. ✅ Notification removed (bell icon disappears)

### Example 3: Changing reminder from "at time" to "1 hour before"

1. Go to Schedule → Tap on supplement
2. Select **"Edit Notification"**
3. Tap **"Change Timing"**
4. Choose **"1 hour before"**
5. ✅ Updated! Now shows "🔔 1 hour before"

## Troubleshooting

### "Permission may be needed" message

If you see this message:
1. Go to your phone's **Settings**
2. Find your app
3. Tap **Notifications**
4. Enable **Allow Notifications**

### Notifications not showing up

1. **Check permission:** Settings → Your App → Notifications → Enabled
2. **Check notification is enabled:** Look for 🔔 icon on scheduled item
3. **Check timing:** Make sure the notification time hasn't passed
4. **Try editing:** Tap supplement → Edit Notification → Change timing

### Can't find the notification option

- It only appears **during scheduling** or when **editing an existing schedule**
- There's no separate notification settings page
- Notifications are tied to each scheduled supplement

## Tips

- ✅ You can have different notification times for different supplements
- ✅ Each scheduled instance can have its own notification settings
- ✅ Notifications will show even if the app is closed
- ✅ Deleting a schedule automatically cancels its notification
- ⚠️ Past notifications won't be scheduled (only future times)
- 💡 The bell icon color is orange (🔔) to make it easy to spot
